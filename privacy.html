Privacy Policy

Sundae Jam, last updated October 19, 2026

Sundae Jam is a puzzle game made by Kettlekite, the studio name of independent developer Mert Can Gözükara. We built it so that it works without knowing who you are.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for an account, name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, the game engine's own analytics and crash reporting are switched off, and it never sends anything to us.

What stays on your device

Your progress (current level, stars, gems, boosters, your Sundae Streak, the daily gift and your sound, music and vibration settings) is saved in one file inside the app on your device. So is the level log you can open in Settings: for each level, your tries and wins, the revives and boosters you used, the hearts left at a win and how full the prep tray got. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, that backup can include this file, under Apple's terms. We never see it.

Notifications and permissions

The game sends no notifications and asks for no permissions.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Gems and boosters you buy are saved with your progress on your device, together with the numbers of your recent purchases, so that a purchase is never added twice.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases can be limited with Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions? Write to support@kettlekite.com.
