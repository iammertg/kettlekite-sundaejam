Privacy Policy

Sundae Jam, last updated November 21, 2026

Sundae Jam is a puzzle game made by Kettlekite, the studio name of independent developer Mert Can Gözükara. We built it so that it works without knowing who you are. This policy covers the game on iPhone, from the App Store, and on Android, from Google Play.

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. The game does not ask for an account, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. It contains no analytics SDK, the game engine’s own analytics and crash reporting are switched off, and it never sends anything to us. We run no server for it.

On iPhone, from version 1.2, the game shows ads. The companies that serve them receive data from your device, as described under Ads. The Android version has no ads.

What stays on your device

Your progress (current level, stars, gems, boosters, your Sundae Streak, the daily gift and your sound, music and vibration settings) is saved in one file inside the app on your device. So are your profile (the nickname you choose, your avatar, frame and achievements), your progress in events and the Sundae Pass, and the level log you can open in Settings: for each level, your tries and wins, the revives and boosters you used, the hearts left at a win and how full the prep tray got. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Events run on your device. Their rivals are computer-controlled, and your nickname and scores are not sent anywhere.

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, or your Android phone backs up its apps’ data to your Google account, that backup can include this file, under Apple’s or Google’s terms. We never see it.

Ads (iPhone)

From version 1.2, ads keep Sundae Jam free on iPhone:

  • ads you choose to watch for a reward: another chance on a lost level, double gems after a win, or a free booster;
  • now and then a short ad between levels, from level 20 on. Never after a lost level, never in your first session, and never within a day of a purchase.

The No Ads purchase removes the ads between levels for good. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

The ads are served by Google AdMob, which picks each ad from Google’s advertisers and from partner ad networks, such as Unity Ads. To show an ad, limit how often you see it, measure it and prevent fraud, these companies receive data from your device:

  • your IP address, from which they estimate a rough location;
  • device and app details, such as the model, iOS version, language, time zone and the game’s version;
  • how you interact with the ads you see;
  • an identifier for this app on your device and, only if you allow tracking, your device’s advertising identifier.

Each company handles this data under its own privacy policy: Google and Unity.

Tracking. iOS asks whether Sundae Jam may track you. If you allow it, the networks also receive your device’s advertising identifier and may show you ads based on what you do in other companies’ apps and websites. If you don’t, they don’t receive it. Your answer changes nothing in the game. You can change it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game, under Settings → Ad Privacy.

We don’t sell your data. The ad networks send us no information that identifies you: we see totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Game Center

From version 1.1, the game uses Game Center, Apple’s game service, for a Highest Level leaderboard and for inviting friends. Game Center is optional: the whole game can be played without it.

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game signs in when it starts (Apple may show its welcome banner), and each level you win sends the level you have reached to the game’s leaderboard. Game Center shows that score according to your Game Center settings.

Invite a Friend opens Apple’s friend request sheet, or Apple’s sign-in sheet if you are not signed in. If neither can show, it opens the share sheet with the game’s App Store link; Share Link sends just the link. You choose who receives it. iOS asks whether the game may see your Game Center friends only when you open Friends in your profile, or tap Show Friends on the card that explains it (shown once, from level 10, with a Later button). If you allow it, the game reads which of your friends play Sundae Jam and the level they have reached, shows them on your device, and keeps the game-specific Game Center IDs of the friends who earned you a free Keep Going, so that each friend counts once. If you decline, the rest of the game works the same. You can change your answer later in iOS Settings.

Your Game Center account, friends and scores are held by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. The game does not send them to us.

Game Center is Apple’s and is not part of the Android version: on Android there is no leaderboard, no friends list and no invitations, and the level you reach is not sent anywhere.

Notifications and permissions

The game sends no notifications. On iPhone, it can ask for two permissions: access to your Game Center friends, described above, and, from version 1.2, whether it may track you for ads, described under Ads. Both are optional, the friends question is never asked during play, and your answers change nothing in the game.

On Android, the game never asks for a permission. The few it holds are granted when it is installed, without a question: Google Play billing, internet and network access (used by Google Play’s billing library; the game itself sends nothing) and vibration, for the haptics.

Purchases

On iPhone, in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

On Android, in-app purchases are processed by Google Play, under Google’s privacy policy. The game uses Google Play’s billing library, which talks to Google Play on your phone to show prices and complete purchases; we never see your payment details or your Google account. If a purchase does not finish (for example, the game closes during it), Google Play keeps it, and the game adds it the next time it starts.

Gems and boosters you buy are saved with your progress on your device, together with the numbers of your recent purchases, so that a purchase is never added twice. Your purchases are not shared with the ad networks.

Children

Sundae Jam is made for a general audience and is not directed to children. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. On iPhone, purchases can be limited with Screen Time and Ask to Buy, and Game Center features such as adding friends with Screen Time. On Android, purchases can be limited with the Play Store’s “Require authentication for purchases” setting and with Family Link’s purchase approvals.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies. Its app-ads.txt file lists the ad networks allowed to sell the game’s ad space.

Changes

If a future version changes how the game handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details and the Google Play Data safety section will change accordingly.
