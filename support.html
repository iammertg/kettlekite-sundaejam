Support

Sundae Jam, a dessert-counter puzzle for iPhone.

Write to support@kettlekite.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened, and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes your levels, stars, gems and boosters, and purchases can’t be restored after that.

Why can’t I tap a dessert?

A dessert slides out only when nothing sits on top of it or blocks its way. Tapping a blocked one costs a heart, and when the hearts run out the level ends, so look before you tap.

What do the boosters do?

The Spoon scoops out any dessert, the Smoothie adds a place to the prep tray and the Blender mixes up the counter.

I bought something but it didn’t arrive

Close the game and open it again: the App Store delivers any purchase that didn’t finish the next time the game starts. If it still isn’t there, write to us.

How do I stop purchases on a child’s iPhone?

Use Screen Time (iOS Settings, then Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions) or Ask to Buy in Family Sharing.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
