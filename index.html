Clear the counter. Serve the sundaes.

A dessert puzzle for iPhone, in development. Tap a scoop and it slides out only if its path is clear, then lands in the bowl that needs it.

In development Write to us

How it plays

Scoops, sauces and toppings sit locked on a kitchen counter. One can leave only when nothing blocks its way out. Every sundae and banana split waits for its own flavours, and anything no bowl needs yet waits in a five-slot prep tray, so don't let it fill up.
