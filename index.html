How it plays

Scoops, bananas, wafer rolls, waffles and brownies are packed on a dessert counter. A dessert slides out only when nothing sits on top of it or blocks its way, and whatever was underneath comes free once it leaves. Desserts no glass wants yet wait in a five-place prep tray, so don't let it fill up.

Tapping a blocked dessert costs a heart; one that slides out costs nothing. 200 levels, from relaxing to truly tricky, and every one is checked solvable. No timers: play at your own pace.

For parents

No account, no ads and no analytics. Progress stays on the iPhone. Purchases go through the App Store, and Screen Time and Ask to Buy can limit them. The privacy policy has the details.
