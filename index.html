How it plays

Read the counter, then tap. No timers: play at your own pace.

  1. Tap a dessert

    Every dessert has an arrow. If its way is clear, it slides off the counter into the glass that wants its flavour.

  2. Use the tray

    Desserts no glass wants yet wait in a five-place prep tray. A tap on a blocked dessert costs a heart.

  3. Serve them all

    Fill every glass and the counter is clear. Wins on the first try grow your Sundae Streak.

Four tricks on the counter.

They arrive one at a time, each on a dessert you can reach long before you need it, so every one changes a decision.

Frozen desserts

Ice holds a dessert in place. It thaws as others leave the counter: the number counts down to zero.

Twin cherries

Two cherries on one stem need two places at once, so plan room for both.

Key and padlock

A padlocked dessert opens only when its key dessert has left the counter.

Turntables

Tap a blocked dessert on a turntable and the table turns instead, for a move, until its way out lines up.

Orders grow from scoop colours to whole desserts: sundaes, banana splits, waffle bowls and brownie sundaes.

A thousand levels.

With hard and super-hard ones, boosters, a daily gift and a journey to climb. Every level is checked solvable before it ships.

Nothing to sign up for.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Purchases go through the App Store, where Screen Time and Ask to Buy can limit them.
